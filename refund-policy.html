Refund & Cancellation Policy

Independent private travel agency: Rooted Journeys provides safari booking assistance and travel arrangements. We are not the Rajasthan Forest Department, a government booking website, or the authority issuing safari permits. All safari permits, timings, zones and entry are subject to official rules and availability.

Last updated: 23 November 2026.

Please read these terms before paying for a booking. Actual refund eligibility depends on whether a permit or other service has already been issued and on the applicable official and supplier conditions communicated for your booking.

1. If we cannot obtain the requested permit

If the requested permit cannot be secured, we will explain available alternatives. If you decline the alternatives, we will refund the amount paid for services not provided, less only genuinely incurred, previously disclosed non-refundable third-party costs where applicable and lawful. We will provide an itemised explanation of deductions.

2. Cancellation before permit issuance

Contact us immediately by phone, WhatsApp or email. We will confirm whether any third-party costs have already been incurred and explain the eligible refund before processing.

3. Cancellation after permit issuance

Once issued, permits are subject to the applicable Forest Department cancellation and modification rules. Refunds are not guaranteed. We will explain the official and previously disclosed agency or supplier deductions that apply to your specific booking.

4. Hotel, transport and other services

Any separate supplier cancellation conditions will be provided when quoting those services. We will not present a supplier fee as a government fee.

5. Refund requests and processing

Send your booking reference, travel date and contact details to us. We will review the request, confirm the eligible amount and expected processing timeline in writing, and return approved refunds through an appropriate payment method in accordance with applicable law. Bank and payment-provider processing times may vary.
