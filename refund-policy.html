Refund & Cancellation Policy

Clear cancellation conditions and guidance on requesting a refund for your safari or travel arrangements.

Last updated · 28 November 2026

Independent private travel agency: Rooted Journeys is not the Rajasthan Forest Department, a government booking website or the authority issuing safari permits. Permits, timings, zones and entry remain subject to official rules and availability.

Please read this policy before paying for a booking. Refund eligibility depends on whether a permit or other service has already been issued and on the applicable official and supplier conditions communicated for your booking.

At a glance: 100% refund if we cannot secure your safari permit, including government and agency service charges. Refund initiated the same day or next day after unsuccessful booking; bank credit times may vary.

1. If we cannot obtain the requested permit

If Rooted Journeys cannot secure the requested safari permit, we will refund 100% of the amount collected for that unsuccessful booking, including government booking charges, agency service fees and any additional booking charges. This applies whether you paid a deposit or the full price. No deductions apply. We initiate the refund on the same day or the next day after the booking is confirmed unsuccessful. The time for funds to reach your account depends on your bank or payment provider.

2. Cancellation before permit issuance

Contact us immediately by phone, WhatsApp or email. We will confirm whether any third-party costs have already been incurred and explain the eligible refund before processing.

3. Cancellation after permit issuance

Once issued, permits are subject to the applicable Forest Department cancellation and modification rules. Refunds are not guaranteed. We will explain the official and previously disclosed agency or supplier deductions that apply to your specific booking.

4. Hotel, transport and other services

Any separate supplier cancellation conditions will be provided when quoting those services. We will not present a supplier fee as a government fee.

5. Refund requests and processing

  1. Send your booking reference, travel date and contact details to us.
  2. For unsuccessful permit bookings, we initiate a full refund on the same day or next day. For other cancellation requests, we confirm eligibility and processing timelines in writing.
  3. Approved refunds will be returned through an appropriate payment method in accordance with applicable law. Bank and payment-provider processing times may vary.

Please do not share payment passwords, PINs or OTPs. See our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions. Nothing in this policy limits mandatory consumer protection rights.
