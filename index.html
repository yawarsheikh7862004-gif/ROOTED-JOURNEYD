Before you travel

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a Ranthambore safari?

Send your preferred date, shift, vehicle and group size using our enquiry form or WhatsApp. We check the available options and provide an itemized quote before payment. Permits are issued through Forest Department channels.

How much does a Ranthambore safari cost?

Our indicative advance shared rates are ₹1,250 for Indian Canter guests, ₹2,450 for Indian Gypsy guests, ₹2,700 for foreign Canter guests and ₹3,900 for foreign Gypsy guests, per seat. Final rates are confirmed before payment.

What is the difference between Gypsy and Canter?

A Gypsy is a six-seat open vehicle, while a Canter is a larger twenty-seat open vehicle. Both are subject to availability and park rules.

Can I request a specific safari zone?

Yes, you can share your preferred zone. Allocation depends on Forest Department rules and availability; a requested zone is not guaranteed.

Are tiger sightings guaranteed?

No. Ranthambore is a natural wildlife habitat, and sightings depend on animal movements and conditions.

Can I enquire about last-minute safari bookings?

Yes. Ask about current booking availability. Current and Tatkal are separate booking categories, and neither guarantees a permit.

Is Rooted Journeys an official government booking portal?

No. Rooted Journeys is an independent private travel agency offering safari booking assistance. You may also book directly through official Forest Department channels.

Can I enquire about a Ranthambhore safari for today or tomorrow?

Yes. Send your date, preferred shift, nationality, vehicle and group size. We can check possible last-minute options and provide a quote, subject to availability.

Are current, Tatkal and Premium Tatkal bookings the same?

No. They are distinct booking categories with different rules and availability. Contact us to discuss which options may apply to your travel date. No category guarantees a permit.

Can Indian and international visitors request a private Jeep safari?

Yes, both can enquire about exclusive Gypsy options. Vehicle availability, applicable nationality-based rates and park rules determine the final quote.
