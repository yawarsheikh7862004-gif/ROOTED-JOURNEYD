Ranthambore Jungle Safari

Ranthambore Safari offers a memorable journey into one of India’s renowned tiger reserves, where dry deciduous forests, open meadows, and ancient fort ruins frame wildlife drives across Zones 1 to 10.

Animals in Ranthambore

Home to the iconic Royal Bengal tiger, Ranthambore also hosts leopards, sloth bears, striped hyenas, jackals, sambar deer, chital, nilgai, wild boar, langurs, crocodiles, and diverse birdlife.

Ranthambore National Park

Located in Sawai Madhopur district of southeastern Rajasthan where the Aravalli and Vindhya ranges meet, Ranthambore is well connected by rail and road and sits roughly 180 km from Jaipur.
