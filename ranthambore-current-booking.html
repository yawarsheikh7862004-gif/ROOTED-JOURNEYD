Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Can I enquire about a safari today or tomorrow?

Yes. Contact Rooted Journeys with your date, shift, group size and vehicle preference. Current booking depends on remaining inventory; an enquiry does not reserve a seat.

What if advance safari booking is sold out?

Ask about current quota and other applicable booking categories, or consider alternative dates and vehicles. No seat is guaranteed.
