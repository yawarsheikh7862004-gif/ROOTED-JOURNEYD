Ranthambore Advance Safari Booking

When does advance booking open?

The published calendar lists opening at noon on 1 August for October–December, 1 November for January–March, and 1 February for April–June.

Planning your visit

Plan early for weekends and holidays. Confirm the applicable season and current rules on the official portal before arranging accommodation.

Source: Ranthambhore Tiger Reserve information portal. Rules and prices may change.
