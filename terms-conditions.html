Terms & Conditions

Clear booking terms, transparent pricing and responsible wildlife travel.

Last updated · 24 November 2026

Independent private travel agency: Rooted Journeys is not the Rajasthan Forest Department, a government booking website or the authority issuing safari permits. Permits, timings, zones and entry remain subject to official rules and availability.

1. Our role

Rooted Journeys is an independent booking facilitator and travel service provider. We do not issue government permits or control Forest Department inventory, routes, shifts or entry decisions.

2. Quotes and payments

Displayed prices are agency-arranged package prices, not government-only tariffs. Before payment, we disclose the estimated official or supplier charges, our agency service fee, included services and the total payable. Any exclusions or applicable additional charges will be explained before you approve the quote. Customers pay Rooted Journeys for the requested arrangements. Do not pay unless you agree to the communicated total and terms.

3. Confirmation and availability

An enquiry, quotation or payment does not guarantee a safari permit. Advance, current and Tatkal booking requests are subject to applicable official rules and availability. A booking is confirmed only after the relevant permit or supplier confirmation is issued and shared. Vehicle type, zone, timing and visitor requirements are governed by the applicable authority and provider rules.

4. Traveller responsibilities

Provide accurate passenger information and carry the identity document required for entry. Follow applicable park, safety, timing and guide instructions. Wildlife sightings, including tigers, cannot be guaranteed. Please respect wildlife and the natural environment.

5. Third-party services

Where accommodation, vehicles or other travel services are arranged, availability and specific conditions will be explained before payment. We will assist with service issues, subject to applicable law and the responsibility of the relevant provider.

6. Changes, cancellations and refunds

See our Refund & Cancellation Policy. If Rooted Journeys cannot secure the requested safari permit, we will refund 100% of the amount collected for that unsuccessful booking, including government booking charges, agency service fees and any additional booking charges. No deductions apply. We initiate the refund on the same day or the next day after the booking is confirmed unsuccessful. The time for funds to reach your account depends on your bank or payment provider. For customer cancellations or other services, applicable non-refundable charges should be disclosed before payment. Government and supplier rules may restrict changes after confirmation.

7. Privacy and personal information

For details about how enquiry and passenger information is handled, and your optional analytics preferences, see our Privacy Policy.

8. Contact and disputes

For booking questions or complaints, contact us using the information below. Indian law applies, subject to mandatory consumer protection rights.
