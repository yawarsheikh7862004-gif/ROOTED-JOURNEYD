Rooted Journeys is an independent booking facilitator and travel service provider. We do not issue government permits or control Forest Department inventory, routes, shifts or entry decisions.

Our displayed prices are agency-arranged package prices, not government-only tariffs. An enquiry, quotation or payment does not guarantee a safari permit. A booking is confirmed only after the relevant permit or supplier confirmation is issued and shared. Availability, vehicle type, zone, timing and visitor requirements are governed by the applicable authority and provider rules.

Provide accurate passenger information and carry the identity document required for entry. Follow all applicable park, safety, timing and guide instructions. We cannot guarantee tiger or other wildlife sightings.
