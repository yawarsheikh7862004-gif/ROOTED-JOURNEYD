Who we are

About Rooted Journeys

Rooted Journeys is a privately operated travel agency based in Sawai Madhopur, Rajasthan, led by proprietor Habiba Sheikh. We help travellers plan Ranthambore visits, compare Gypsy and Canter safari options, request permits through the applicable official booking system and arrange requested local travel services.

Local travel assistance

Explore safari options, travel dates, zones and practical trip arrangements with guidance from our Sawai Madhopur-based agency.

Transparent pricing

We explain estimated third-party costs and our agency service fee before payment, so you can make an informed decision.

From enquiry to confirmation

How our booking assistance works

01 / PLAN

Share your preferences

Tell us your travel date, group size, nationality and preferred safari.

02 / REVIEW

Receive an itemized quote

We explain available options and the full agency package quote, including our service fee, before payment.

03 / REQUEST

Booking assistance

After approval and receipt of required information, we attempt booking through the official system, subject to availability.

04 / CONFIRM

Receive permit details

We share confirmation once a permit is actually issued. An enquiry or payment alone is not permit confirmation.

Independent private travel agency. We are not the Rajasthan Forest Department, a government booking website or the authority issuing permits. Permits, timings, zones and entry are subject to official rules and availability. Wildlife sightings, including tigers, cannot be guaranteed. We encourage responsible travel and adherence to park rules.
