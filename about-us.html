About Us

Independent private travel agency: Rooted Journeys provides safari booking assistance and travel arrangements. We are not the Rajasthan Forest Department, a government booking website, or the authority issuing safari permits. All safari permits, timings, zones and entry are subject to official rules and availability.

Rooted Journeys is a privately operated travel service based in Sawai Madhopur, Rajasthan. We help travellers plan Ranthambore visits, compare Gypsy and Canter options, request safari permits through the applicable official booking system, and arrange requested local travel services.

How our booking assistance works

  1. Tell us your preferred travel date, group size, nationality, and safari preference.
  2. We explain available options and give you the full agency package quote, including our service charge, before payment.
  3. After you approve the quote and provide required information, we attempt the booking through the official system subject to availability.
  4. We share confirmation details once a permit is actually issued. An enquiry or payment alone is not permit confirmation.

Wildlife sightings, including tigers, cannot be guaranteed. We encourage responsible travel and adherence to park rules.
