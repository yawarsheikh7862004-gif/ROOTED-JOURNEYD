About Us

Independent private travel agency: Rooted Journeys (Proprietor: Habiba Sheikh) provides safari booking assistance and travel arrangements. We are not the Rajasthan Forest Department, a government booking website, or the authority issuing safari permits. Official base charges (approx. ₹971 Indian Canter, ₹2,450 Foreigner Canter, ₹1,627 Indian Gypsy, ₹3,050 Foreigner Gypsy per seat) and our agency service fee are disclosed separately before payment. GST is not applicable to our business.

Rooted Journeys is a privately operated travel agency based in Sawai Madhopur, Rajasthan. Led by Proprietor Habiba Sheikh and registered under Udyam Registration UDYAM-RJ-29-0045322 (NIC 79110 – Travel agency activities), we assist travelers with Ranthambore National Park safari planning, Gypsy and Canter comparisons, and official permit enquiry assistance.

How Our Booking Assistance Works

  1. Share your preferred safari date, shift (morning or afternoon), vehicle type (Gypsy or Canter), nationality, and group size.
  2. We check current availability rules and send you a clear written quote separating the approximate official Forest Department base charges (permit, vehicle, and guide) from Rooted Journeys' agency service fee before requesting any payment.
  3. Once you review and approve the written quote and provide required passenger ID details, we submit the booking request through the official Rajasthan Forest Department portal subject to availability.
  4. We send your official safari permit confirmation once issued. An enquiry or payment alone does not constitute a confirmed permit until officially issued.

Wildlife sightings, including tigers, depend entirely on nature and cannot be guaranteed. We encourage responsible wildlife tourism and adherence to all park guidelines.
