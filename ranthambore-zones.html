Which safari zone is best?

The reserve has ten safari zones. Zone 3 includes Padam Talab and Rajbagh; zone 4 includes Lakarda and Berda; zone 6 includes Kundal and Mansarovar.

Planning your visit

Choose an available zone based on your interests, access and travel logistics. Tigers move naturally; no particular zone can guarantee a sighting.
