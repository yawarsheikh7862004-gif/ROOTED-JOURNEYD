When does Tatkal booking open?

The published calendar lists regular Tatkal at 11 AM for visits seven days ahead. Premium Tatkal is listed separately for three days ahead, subject to cancellation inventory, at 10 AM and 6 PM.

Planning your visit

Check live availability and applicable fees directly. Neither category guarantees a seat. All times are Indian Standard Time.

Choosing between Tatkal and Premium Tatkal

For a Ranthambore Tatkal booking enquiry, share the intended safari date, nationality, group size and vehicle preference. Are Tatkal and Premium Tatkal the same?

No. They are separate booking categories with different release windows and availability conditions. Confirm the applicable rules for your travel date before making payment.

Yes. Send your travel date, visitor count, nationality and preferred vehicle to Rooted Journeys. Availability and charges must be confirmed; booking is not guaranteed.
