Ranthambore Tatkal Safari Booking

When does Tatkal booking open?

The published calendar lists regular Tatkal at 11 AM for visits seven days ahead. Premium Tatkal is listed separately for three days ahead, subject to cancellation inventory, at 10 AM and 6 PM.
