How do I book a safari?

Send Rooted Journeys your preferred date, shift, vehicle and group size for a free enquiry. The quote lists the official fee, our agency fee, any supplier charges and the total. If you accept, you pay Rooted Journeys the full amount or the deposit specified in your quote and provide the passenger details needed for the request. The Rajasthan Forest Department issues permits, subject to availability. We send your confirmation once the permit is issued.

You can also book directly through the Rajasthan Forest Department booking page.

Planning your visit

Advance and Current bookings depend on permit availability. Your safari is confirmed only when the Rajasthan Forest Department issues the permit. Coordinate your hotel, transport and arrival time with the confirmed safari details. Rooted Journeys can help you plan your trip independently; permits are issued through the official system.

For peak dates, ask for a written quote. Agency or supplier charges can be higher during busy periods. Payment terms.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How can I enquire about a Ranthambhore safari online?

Share your travel date, group size, nationality, preferred vehicle and morning or afternoon shift with Rooted Journeys. We can explain available travel assistance and provide a quote. Permits remain subject to official availability and rules.

What should I prepare before booking?

Keep the visitor names and valid identification ready. Confirm the reporting location, total charges and cancellation terms before payment.

If Rooted Journeys cannot secure the requested safari permit, we will refund 100% of the amount collected for that unsuccessful booking, including government booking charges, agency service fees and any additional booking charges.
