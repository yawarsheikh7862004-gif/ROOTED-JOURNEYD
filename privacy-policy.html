Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal information, safari enquiries and website privacy choices.

Last updated · 24 November 2026

Rooted Journeys, operated by Habiba Sheikh, is an independent private travel agency. This policy explains how we handle information shared through rootedjourneys.online, WhatsApp, telephone and email.

1. Information we collect

Depending on your enquiry, we may receive your name, contact details, preferred travel dates, nationality, group size and safari preferences. After you approve a quote, we may request passenger names, ages, gender and government ID or passport details required for official permit processing. Please do not send identity documents before they are needed.

2. How we use information

We use information to prepare itemized quotes, check availability, process permit requests, coordinate requested travel arrangements, communicate booking updates, handle cancellations and refunds, and maintain necessary business records.

3. Information sharing

Required passenger details may be shared with the official booking system and relevant service providers where necessary to arrange your trip. Website hosting, communication and optional analytics providers may also process information as described below. We do not sell, rent or trade personal information to third-party marketers. Information may be disclosed where required by law.

4. Google Analytics and cookies

We use optional Google Analytics 4 to understand website visits and interactions, including clicks on contact buttons and safari enquiry tools. Analytics may process device and browser information, approximate location, page visits, referral information and cookie identifiers. We do not intentionally send names, contact details, identity documents or enquiry message contents to Analytics.

Our website stores your analytics preference locally in your browser. Analytics is intended to start only when you accept it. Google Analytics may set cookies such as _ga and _ga_* after acceptance. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled in our website tag configuration.

You can decline analytics without losing access to our booking assistance. Change your preference using Privacy settings, or clear website cookies through your browser. Withdrawal stops future tracking through our website tag; it does not automatically erase previously collected information.

Learn more about Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from websites.

5. WhatsApp and external services

Our enquiry form prepares a message and opens WhatsApp. The form itself does not confirm a booking or collect payment. When you use WhatsApp, email or an external booking website, those services process information under their own privacy policies. Our hosting provider may process technical request logs for website operation and security.

6. Retention and security

We retain passenger ID details only as long as necessary for booking and applicable legal record-keeping requirements. Other information may be retained as necessary for customer support, disputes and business records. We aim to limit access to information to those who need it for these purposes. No online communication method can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your privacy choices

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of your personal information, or to raise a privacy concern. Requests are subject to applicable law, identity verification where appropriate and necessary record retention. For children travelling with a group, a parent or guardian should provide required booking information.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or data handling practices change. The latest version and revision date will be published on this page.
