Privacy Policy

Independent private travel agency: Rooted Journeys (Proprietor: Habiba Sheikh) provides safari booking assistance and travel arrangements. We are not the Rajasthan Forest Department, a government booking website, or the authority issuing safari permits. Official base charges (approx. ₹971 Indian Canter, ₹2,450 Foreigner Canter, ₹1,627 Indian Gypsy, ₹3,050 Foreigner Gypsy per seat) and our agency service fee are disclosed separately before payment. GST is not applicable to our business.

Last updated: 23 November 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how Rooted Journeys (rootedjourneys.online) collects, uses, and protects personal information shared via our website, WhatsApp, phone, or email.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry, we collect your name, phone/WhatsApp number, email address, preferred travel dates, nationality, and group size. Only after you approve a quote for permit processing do we request passenger details required by the official Rajasthan Forest Department portal (full name, age, gender, and valid Government ID/Passport number).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to prepare itemized quotes, check safari availability, process permit applications on the official booking portal, send booking confirmations, handle refund/cancellation requests, and maintain legally required business records.

3. Data Sharing & Protection

We share only the mandatory passenger details required to generate your safari permit on the official Forest Department system or coordinate confirmed local transport. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketers.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

Passenger ID details are retained only as long as necessary to fulfill your safari booking and comply with applicable Indian record-keeping laws. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us.
